Music Videos
When ON, the prayer overlay applies to music videos (they're treated like any other video). When OFF (default), music videos are exempt from the prayer overlay.
When ON (default), music videos are detected and only the video (picture) is hidden while the audio keeps playing. Detection uses the video category, the <meta> genre, the title (including "official video"), whether the video is auto-generated, and whether the artist has a verified-music badge. Comments are hidden along with the video. When OFF, no video is disabled.
